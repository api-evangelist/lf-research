For organizations offering products with digital elements in the EU, the next major Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) deadline arrives on 11 September 2026. From that date, organizations covered by the reporting obligations must be ready to assess actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe security incidents, coordinate an internal response and submit notifications within the required timelines.
